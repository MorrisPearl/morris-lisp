HOW TO MAKE A LIST OF EVERYONE WHO EMAILS YOU
USING GOOGLE TAKEOUT AND GOOGLE COLAB (nothing to download or install)

WHAT THIS DOES
Google lets you save a complete copy of your email into your own Google
Drive (this is called "Google Takeout"). Then a small program, running on
Google's computers through a free tool called "Google Colab", reads who
each message is from and writes a spreadsheet file with one row per
person: first name, last name, and email address.

Nothing is downloaded to your laptop, and your email never leaves your
Google account. All you do is click buttons in your web browser.

HOW LONG IT TAKES
- Google preparing the copy of your email: anywhere from a few minutes to
  a day or more. You can do other things while you wait; Google will send
  you an email when it is done.
- Running the program in Colab: usually 10 to 30 minutes.

BEFORE YOU START — CHECK YOUR GOOGLE DRIVE SPACE
The copy of your email takes up room in your Google Drive, about as much
as your mailbox itself (it can be many gigabytes). Go to drive.google.com
and look at the storage figure at the bottom of the left-hand column. If
you are close to your limit, tell Morris before you start. You will
delete the copy when you are finished (Part 6).

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — ASK GOOGLE TO SAVE YOUR EMAIL INTO YOUR DRIVE
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your web browser and go to:

   takeout.google.com

   Sign in with your work Google account if asked.

2. Near the top, click "Deselect all". (Otherwise it will try to
   copy everything Google has about you.)

3. Scroll down to "Mail" and tick the box next to it.

4. If you see a button that says "All Mail data included", you can leave
   it as it is. (Optional: click it to untick "Spam" and "Trash" so the
   copy is smaller. The program skips those anyway.)

5. Scroll all the way down and click "Next step".

6. Choose these settings (the wording may be slightly different):
      - Destination:  "Add to Drive"
      - Frequency:    "Export once"
      - File type:    ".zip"
      - File size:    the biggest one offered (for example 50 GB)

7. Click "Create export". Google may ask you to type your password
   again.

   If you do not see "Mail", "Add to Drive", or "Create export", or
   Google says the feature is turned off, your organization's Google
   administrator has disabled Takeout. Tell Morris.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — WAIT FOR GOOGLE
------------------------------------------------------------
8. Google will email you when the copy is ready. This can take from a
   few minutes to a day or more.

9. When the email arrives, go to drive.google.com. In "My Drive" you
   should see a new folder called "Takeout". Open it. Inside are one or
   more files ending in .zip. Do not open them or move them — the
   program reads them where they are.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — OPEN THE PROGRAM IN COLAB
------------------------------------------------------------
10. Click this link (or copy it and paste it into the address bar at the
    top of your browser, then press Return):

    https://colab.research.google.com/github/MorrisPearl/morris-lisp/blob/main/gmail_from_header_export/senders_from_takeout.ipynb

11. Look at the top right of the page. Make sure you are signed in with
    your work Google account (if you see a "Sign in" button, click it).

    If Google says Colab is not available for your account, your
    administrator needs to turn on "Colab" for your organization. Tell
    Morris.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 4 — RUN IT, ONE BOX AT A TIME
------------------------------------------------------------
The page is made of text and grey boxes. Each grey box has a round play
button (a circle with a triangle) at its left edge. You click the play
buttons one at a time, from the top of the page to the bottom. After you
click one, wait for it to finish — a green check mark appears next to
it — before clicking the next one.

12. STEP 1 box ("Connect to your Google Drive"): click its play button.
    - A message may say "This notebook was not authored by Google".
      Click "Run anyway".
    - A pop-up asks permission to access your Google Drive. Click
      "Connect to Google Drive", choose your work account, then click
      "Continue" or "Allow".

13. STEP 2 box ("Find the Takeout export"): click its play button. It
    should print the names and sizes of your .zip files. If instead it
    says it could not find any, see "If something goes wrong" below.

14. STEP 3 box ("Get the program"): click its play button. It should
    print "Got mbox_senders.py".

15. STEP 4 box ("Make the list"): click its play button. This is the
    long one. Progress lines appear as it goes, like:

      50,000 messages read (16% of this file), 33,828 unique senders so far

    Keep this browser tab open, keep your laptop awake and plugged in,
    and wait until you see a line starting with "Wrote". This can take
    30 minutes or more for a very large mailbox.

16. STEP 5 box ("Take a look"): click its play button. It shows a small
    table of the first 20 people on the list.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5 — PUT THE LIST IN GOOGLE SHEETS
------------------------------------------------------------
17. Go to drive.google.com. In "My Drive" (not inside the Takeout
    folder) you will find a file called senders.csv.

18. Right-click senders.csv, choose "Open with", then "Google Sheets".

The sheet has four columns: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and the
original "From" text (this last column is there so you can check that
the names and addresses were split correctly). The newest senders are at
the top.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 6 — DELETE THE COPY OF YOUR EMAIL
------------------------------------------------------------
19. The "Takeout" folder in your Drive holds a complete copy of all your
    email. Once the list is safely in Google Sheets, in Google Drive
    right-click the "Takeout" folder, choose "Remove" (or "Move to
    trash"), then click "Trash" on the left side of Drive and click
    "Empty trash".

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
- Step 2 says it could not find any .zip files: the Takeout copy may not
  be finished yet (wait for Google's email), or it may have been sent
  somewhere other than Google Drive. Look for a "Takeout" folder in
  drive.google.com.
- Step 4 stops or says "disconnected": the Colab page lost its
  connection (often because the laptop went to sleep or the tab was
  closed). Just start again at the STEP 1 box and click the play buttons
  in order again. It starts over, so it takes the same time again.
- Colab says you have reached a usage limit: wait a few hours and try
  again.
- If you see any other error, take a screenshot (on a Mac press
  Command+Shift+4, then click and drag over the part of the screen to
  capture) and send it to Morris.
