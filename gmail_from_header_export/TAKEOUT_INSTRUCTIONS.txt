HOW TO MAKE A LIST OF EVERYONE WHO EMAILS YOU, USING GOOGLE TAKEOUT
(Instructions for Mac laptops)

WHAT THIS DOES
Google lets you download a complete copy of your email (this is called
"Google Takeout"). A small program then reads who each message is from
and makes a spreadsheet file with one row per person: first name, last
name, and email address. Everything happens on your own Mac — your email
is not sent to anyone.

HOW LONG IT TAKES
- Google preparing your download: anywhere from a few minutes to a day
  or more. You can do other things while you wait; Google will send you
  an email when it is ready.
- Running the program on your Mac: usually 5 to 30 minutes.

IMPORTANT — PRIVACY
The file you download contains the full text of ALL of your email and
all attachments. Do not email it, upload it anywhere, or share it. You
will delete it at the end (Part 6).

------------------------------------------------------------
A FEW MAC BASICS YOU'LL USE BELOW
------------------------------------------------------------
- Copy:  hold Command (the ⌘ key next to the space bar) and press C
- Paste: hold Command and press V
- To open Terminal (a window where you type commands): hold Command and
  press the Space bar, type the word Terminal, and press Return.
- Finder is the smiling blue face icon at the left end of the row of
  icons at the bottom of your screen. It shows your files and folders.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — ASK GOOGLE FOR YOUR EMAIL
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your web browser (Safari or Chrome) and go to:

   takeout.google.com

   Sign in with your work Google account if asked.

2. Near the top, click "Deselect all". (Otherwise it will try to
   download everything Google has about you.)

3. Scroll down to "Mail" and tick the box next to it.

4. If you see a button that says "All Mail data included", you can leave
   it as it is. (Optional: click it to untick "Spam" and "Trash" so the
   download is smaller. The program skips those anyway.)

5. Scroll all the way down and click "Next step".

6. Choose these settings (the wording may be slightly different):
      - Destination:  "Send download link via email"
      - Frequency:    "Export once"
      - File type:    ".zip"
      - File size:    the biggest one offered (for example 50 GB)

7. Click "Create export". Google may ask you to type your password
   again.

8. Now wait. Google will email you when the download is ready. Google
   keeps the finished download on its own computers for about a week, so
   you do not need to hurry — but do not forget about it.

   If you do not see "Mail" or "Create export", or Google says the
   feature is turned off, your organization's Google administrator has
   disabled Takeout. Tell Morris — there is another way to do this.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — DOWNLOAD IT
------------------------------------------------------------
9. When the email arrives, open it and click "Download your files".
   Sign in again if asked.

10. Click the download button. The file goes to your "Downloads" folder.
    A large mailbox may come as more than one file — download each one.
    The files can be very large (many gigabytes), so make sure your
    laptop is plugged in and has enough free space, and be patient.

11. In Finder, click "Downloads" in the left column. If the download is
    a file ending in .zip, double-click it to open it (Safari may have
    already done this for you). This creates a folder called "Takeout".
    Do this for every .zip file if there are several.

12. Inside, open the folders: Takeout, then Mail. You should see a file
    ending in .mbox, probably named
    "All mail Including Spam and Trash.mbox".
    Leave Finder open on this folder.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — GET THE PROGRAM
------------------------------------------------------------
13. Open Terminal (hold Command, press Space, type Terminal, press
    Return). A window opens with a blinking cursor.

14. Type this (or copy and paste it) and press Return:

    cd ~/Downloads

15. Then copy and paste this whole line and press Return:

    curl -O https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MorrisPearl/morris-lisp/main/gmail_from_header_export/mbox_senders.py

    You should see a short progress table, and a file called
    mbox_senders.py now exists in your Downloads folder.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 4 — RUN THE PROGRAM
------------------------------------------------------------
16. In the Terminal window, type this, including the space at the end,
    but DO NOT press Return yet:

    python3 mbox_senders.py

17. Now, in Finder, click on the .mbox file and drag it into the
    Terminal window, then let go of the mouse button. Terminal fills in
    the file's location for you. (If you have several .mbox files, drag
    each one in the same way.)

18. Press Return.

    - FIRST TIME ONLY: a box may pop up saying "The 'python3' command
      requires the command line developer tools". Click "Install",
      agree to the terms, and wait for it to finish (this can take
      several minutes). Then go back to Terminal, press the Up-arrow
      key to bring back the line you typed, and press Return again.

19. The program prints progress lines like:

      50,000 messages read (16% of this file), 33,828 unique senders so far

    Let it run until it prints a line starting with "Wrote". Do not
    close Terminal or let your laptop go to sleep. A very large mailbox
    can take 30 minutes or more.

20. When it finishes, a file called senders.csv has been created in the
    same folder as the .mbox file (the "Mail" folder from step 12).

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5 — PUT THE LIST IN GOOGLE SHEETS
------------------------------------------------------------
21. In your browser go to sheets.google.com and click "Blank".

22. Click "File", then "Import", then the "Upload" tab.

23. Drag senders.csv from Finder into the box (or click "Browse" and
    pick it).

24. Choose "Replace spreadsheet" and click "Import data".

The sheet has four columns: First Name, Last Name, Email Address, and
the original "From" text (this last column is there so you can check
that the names and addresses were split correctly). The newest senders
are at the top.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 6 — DELETE THE BIG FILES
------------------------------------------------------------
25. Your complete email is sitting on your Mac. Once the list is in
    Google Sheets, drag the "Takeout" folder and the .zip file(s) from
    your Downloads folder to the Trash, then right-click the Trash icon
    in the Dock and choose "Empty Trash".

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
- "No such file or directory": the .mbox file's location wasn't filled
  in properly. Try step 16-17 again, and make sure there is a space
  between "mbox_senders.py" and the dragged-in file.
- Terminal seems frozen: it is probably still working. Progress lines
  appear every 50,000 messages.
- If you see any other error, take a screenshot (press
  Command+Shift+4, then click and drag over the part of the screen to
  capture) and send it to Morris. Please send only the screenshot —
  not the email files.
