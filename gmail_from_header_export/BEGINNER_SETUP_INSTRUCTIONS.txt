HOW TO SET UP THE GMAIL SENDER LIST TOOL
(Instructions for Mac laptops)

WHAT THIS DOES
This is a small tool that looks through your Gmail and creates a brand-new
Google Sheet listing everyone who has ever sent you email: their first name,
last name, and email address, one row per person. You only need to set it
up once. Setting it up takes about 10 minutes. After that, the tool works
on its own for as long as it needs (possibly many hours or a few days for a
very large mailbox) — you do not need to keep your laptop open.

------------------------------------------------------------
A FEW MAC BASICS YOU'LL USE BELOW
------------------------------------------------------------
- The "Command" key is the key next to the space bar with a little
  clover-looking symbol (⌘). Most shortcuts below use it together with
  another key — hold Command down, tap the other key once, then let go
  of both.
- Select All: hold Command and press A
- Copy:       hold Command and press C
- Paste:      hold Command and press V
- Save:       hold Command and press S
- To switch between two browser tabs, just click on the tab you want
  near the top of the browser window.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — COPY THE FIRST FILE
------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open your web browser (Safari or Chrome — whichever icon you normally
   click in the row of icons at the bottom of your screen).

2. Click this link (or copy it and paste it into the address bar at the
   top of the browser, then press Return):

   https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MorrisPearl/morris-lisp/main/gmail_from_header_export/Code.gs

   You'll see a page full of plain text/code. That's expected.

3. Click anywhere in that text, then press Command+A to select all of it
   (it will turn blue/highlighted), then press Command+C to copy it.

   Keep this browser tab open — you'll come back to it.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — CREATE A NEW GOOGLE SHEET
------------------------------------------------------------
4. Open a new browser tab (click the small "+" next to your open tabs,
   or press Command+T).

5. Go to: sheets.google.com
   Make sure you are signed in with your work Google account
   (mpearl@patrioticmillionaires.org or your own org address).

6. Click "Blank" to create a new, empty spreadsheet.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — OPEN THE SCRIPT EDITOR
------------------------------------------------------------
7. At the top of the spreadsheet, click "Extensions", then click
   "Apps Script". A new tab will open — this is the script editor,
   it looks very different from the spreadsheet.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 4 — PASTE IN THE FIRST FILE
------------------------------------------------------------
8. In the script editor, you'll see a file called "Code.gs" already open
   with some sample text in it.

9. Click inside that text area, press Command+A to select everything
   that's already there, then press the Delete key to remove it.

10. Press Command+V to paste in the text you copied in Part 1, step 3.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5 — ADD THE SECOND FILE
------------------------------------------------------------
11. Go back to the browser tab with Part 1 open, and go to this link
    the same way you did before:

    https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MorrisPearl/morris-lisp/main/gmail_from_header_export/Dialog.html

    Click in the page, Command+A to select all, Command+C to copy.

12. Switch back to the script editor tab.

13. On the left side, find the word "Files" with a "+" next to it.
    Click the "+", then click "HTML".

14. It will ask you to name the file. Type exactly:

    Dialog

    (just that word, nothing else) and press Return.

15. A new file opens with some sample text already in it. Click inside
    it, press Command+A to select it all, press Delete to remove it,
    then press Command+V to paste in the text you copied in step 11.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 6 — SAVE
------------------------------------------------------------
16. Press Command+S to save. If it asks you to name the project, you can
    type anything you like, for example "Sender Export", then
    click OK.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 7 — START THE TOOL
------------------------------------------------------------
17. Switch back to the browser tab with your Google Sheet in it, and
    reload the page (press Command+R, or click the circular arrow
    button near the address bar).

18. After it reloads, look at the menu bar near the top of the sheet
    (next to "File", "Edit", "View"...). You should now see a new menu
    called "Sender Export". Click it, then click "Start export...".

19. The FIRST time you do this, Google will ask permission for the
    tool to read your Gmail, create Sheets, and keep running in the
    background. This is expected — click through the prompts:
      - Click "Continue" or "Allow"
      - Choose your work Google account if asked
      - You may see a screen that says "Google hasn't verified this
        app" — this is normal for a private tool like this one that
        only you are using (it's not published publicly). Click
        "Advanced", then click "Go to [project name] (unsafe)",
        then click "Allow" to finish.
    After you allow it, click "Start export..." in the menu again.

------------------------------------------------------------
PART 8 — USE IT
------------------------------------------------------------
20. A small box will pop up with some choices:
      - "What to search" — leave as "All Mail" to cover everything, or
        choose "Inbox only", or pick one of your Gmail labels from
        the list.
      - "Date range" — optional. Leave blank to include everything.
      - A checkbox for "Skip senders that are not people" — already
        checked. It leaves out things like info@ and no-reply@.

21. Click the "Start Export" button, and keep the box open for the
    first few minutes (up to about 5). Then it will show a message
    saying how far it got, and a link that says "Open the results
    sheet".

22. If the message says "Still running", that's normal for a big
    mailbox. You can close the box, close the tab, even close your
    laptop. The tool keeps working on its own on Google's computers.
    The list of people grows in the results sheet as it goes, starting
    with the newest email and working back.

23. To check on it later, open your original Google Sheet (the one with
    the "Sender Export" menu), click "Sender Export", then
    "Show progress". It says how many people it has found so far.
    When it says "Finished", the list is complete.

    Please do not click "Start export..." a second time while it is
    still running.

------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
------------------------------------------------------------
- The most common mistake is naming the second file something other
  than exactly "Dialog" — double check that in Part 5, step 14.
- If "Show progress" mentions an error about "too many times" or
  "quota", that is Google's daily limit. Do nothing — the tool waits
  and tries again by itself.
- If you see red error text anywhere else, take a screenshot (press
  Command+Shift+4, then click and drag over the error to capture it)
  and send it to Morris.
